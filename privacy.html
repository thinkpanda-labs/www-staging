Privacy policy

Thinkpanda Limited · Effective 26 Nov 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Thinkpanda collects, uses and protects personal information. Effective date: 26 Nov 2026.

Thinkpanda is operated by Thinkpanda Limited, Unit 22C, 235 Wing Lok Street, Sheungwan, Hongkong ("Thinkpanda", "we", "us"). We help hardware founders verify factories, customize products and check production quality.

This policy covers:

  • our website, thinkpanda.com
  • the "Pitch us your build" form
  • emails, calls and meetings with us
  • the projects we run for clients

It does not cover other websites we link to, such as LinkedIn.

Questions? Email privacy@thinkpanda.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to reply to you and run your project.

Where it comes fromWhat we collect
Pitch formName and email (required); company and funding type (optional); project stage; region; your message
Emails, calls and meetingsYour contact details, what you tell us, our notes, and files you share such as briefs, drawings and renders
Client projectsContact and billing details, signed agreements (including NDAs), specs, reports, photos and videos from factory visits
Your visit to the websiteIP address and basic browser details, received by our web host when the page loads

Please don't send us sensitive personal information, such as health or identity documents. We don't need it.

Your product designs and business information are also protected by the NDA we sign with you.

3. How we use it

We use your information only for the purposes below. Where a law asks for a legal basis, it is shown beside each purpose.

PurposeLegal basis
Reply to your pitch and send a confirmation emailSteps you asked for before a contract
Run your project: verification, customization, production checksOur contract with you
Share your requirements with factories and suppliersOur contract with you, and only after you agree
Invoices, tax and accountingLegal obligation
Keep the website and form safe from spam and misuseOur legitimate interest in a working, secure service

We don't sell personal information. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only when we need to, and only what is needed.

  • Our team. Team members who work on your project, under confidentiality duties.
  • Service providers. Google (Google Workspace and Google Apps Script) receives form entries and handles our email. Our web host stores and serves the website. They act on our instructions.
  • Factories and suppliers. During a project, we share only what each one needs, after you agree. We use written agreements with them where appropriate.
  • Professional advisers. Lawyers, accountants and auditors, under confidentiality duties.
  • Authorities. When the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A buyer of our business. If Thinkpanda is sold or merged, the new owner must follow this policy.

5. International transfers

Our team works in Guangzhou, Dongguan, Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Taipei, with staff in Canada and Europe. Your information may be accessed from these places. Our service providers may store it in other countries, including the United States.

When information leaves the country where it was collected, we protect it by:

  • using contract terms that the law accepts, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses
  • following the cross-border transfer rules of mainland China when they apply
  • giving access only to people who need it for your project

You can ask us for more detail about these safeguards.

6. How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep information only as long as we need it.

InformationHow long we keep it
Pitches that don't become a project24 months after our last contact, then deleted
Client project and billing recordsDuring the project, then 7 years for legal, tax and accounting needs
Signed NDAsAs long as their duties last

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), two-factor sign-in on team accounts, and access limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • limit how we use it, or object to a use
  • give you a copy in a common file format
  • stop a use you agreed to, at any time

To make a request, email privacy@thinkpanda.com. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. Requests are usually free.

You can also complain to your privacy regulator. Examples are your national data protection authority in the EU, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and Hong Kong's Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data. We would like the chance to fix the issue first.

8. Cookies, fonts and links

Our website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Fonts. We host our fonts ourselves, so loading the page sends nothing to Google.
  • Spam protection. The form uses a hidden field to stop bots. It sets no cookies.
  • Links. Our LinkedIn page and other linked sites follow their own privacy policies.

9. Children, changes and contact

Children. Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes. We will post any update on this page with a new effective date. If a change is important, we will also email our current clients.
