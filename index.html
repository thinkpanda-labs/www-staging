SHT 02 / 12The gap · 缺口

Finding a factory is the easy part.

Online marketplaces, trade fairs and referrals can give you a supplier list in a week. After that, three questions decide whether your product ships on time.

?01 · 信任

Can you trust them?

A good website and fast replies prove very little. Someone needs to visit the factory, check the business licence and inspect the samples before you send a deposit.

!We verify in person.

?02 · 溝通

Do they understand what you mean?

Your product lives in a pitch deck and a few renders. A factory works from drawings, tolerances and a bill of materials. Details get lost in between, and you find out at the sample stage.

!We write bilingual EN/中文 specs.

?03 · 定製

Do you need to build from scratch?

Most first products do not. An existing ODM/OEM product with the right changes ships faster, costs less and carries less risk than a full custom build.

!We customize what already works.

SHT 05 / 12Process · 流程DETAIL B

How a customization project runs.

Seven steps from your first message to boxes leaving the factory. You get a written output at every step, so you always know where the project stands.

You provide
A brief, a target price and volume, your launch date
We provide
Factory shortlist, spec pack, mockups, QC reports
Languages
English · Deutsch · 中文 · 日本語
Duration
2-8 weeks
  1. 01

    Brief 需求簡報

    You tell us what you want to sell, to whom, at what price and in what volume. We ask the questions a factory will ask later.

    Output →One-page brief
  2. 02

    Factory shortlist 工廠篩選

    We match your brief to existing ODM/OEM products and the factories that make them. Then we verify the best two or three.

    Output →ShortlistVerify reports
  3. 03

    Samples 樣品

    Stock samples go to you and to our Shenzhen workbench. We test them side by side and list what must change.

    Output →Sample evaluation
  4. 04

    Modification spec 修改規格

    We write every change into a bilingual EN/中文 spec pack: drawings, CMF references, firmware behaviour, packaging and acceptance criteria.

    Output →Spec pack v1
  5. 05

    3D-printed mockup 3D 打印樣件

    Our in-house printers turn the spec into something you can hold. We fix fit, feel and proportions before the factory cuts any tooling.

    Output →MockupPhotos and video
  6. 06

    Golden sample 金樣

    The factory builds one unit to the spec. We inspect it, you approve it, and both sides sign it. It becomes the reference for every unit after it.

    Output →Signed golden sampleApproved簽樣
  7. 07

    Production QC 生產質檢

    We check incoming parts, the production line and finished goods against the golden sample, and report to you before anything ships.

    Output →QC reportsQC pass質檢合格

SHT 11 / 12FAQ · 常見問題

Questions founders ask us.

Do not see yours? Ask us directly. We answer in plain language.

Q01How much does it cost?

Verification is fixed-price, so you know the cost before we start. Customization projects are quoted after the brief, based on the number of changes and sample rounds. Full development is scoped per project.

Current prices: Verify is $2,560, Customize starts at $6,280, and Watch is $1,440 a month

Q02How long does a project take?

A Verify report usually arrives within a week after the visit is booked. A customization project from brief to golden sample usually takes 2-8 weeks, depending on the factory and the number of sample rounds. You get a dated plan after the brief.

Q03How do you protect my intellectual property?

We sign a non-disclosure agreement with you before you share details. With factories, we use agreements written for Chinese courts where appropriate, share only what each supplier needs, and recommend you register your trademarks in China early.

We are not a law firm. For legal protection, we work alongside your lawyer.

Q04What minimum order quantities (MOQs) should I expect?

Starting from an existing ODM product usually means lower MOQs than a custom build, because the tooling already exists. Colour and logo changes often have lower thresholds than new tooling. We negotiate MOQs during the shortlist and give you real numbers before you commit.

Q05How do we communicate?

You get one bilingual point of contact who speaks with you in English and with the factory in Chinese. Specs are written in both languages, side by side. Updates come in writing, with a written report at each milestone.

Q06What about time zones?

Our team works 10:00 to 18:00 daily, in HKT (Hong Kong Time) and CET (Central European Time). At the start of a project we agree fixed call windows that suit North America, Western Europe or the Middle East. Between calls, written updates mean you often start your day with progress already made.

Q07What does "verified" mean?

A verified factory has passed our checklist: business licence and registration confirmed, an in-person visit by our team, production capability matched to your product, samples inspected, and a risk score with notes.

It means we saw it ourselves. It does not guarantee future performance, which is why we offer Watch.

Q08When does full development (EVT, DVT, PVT) make sense?

When no existing product can be changed to meet your needs, when your advantage is in the core hardware, or when your volumes justify custom tooling. We usually get there after a Verify and a Customize project together, when both sides know how the other works.

Q09Can you work with a factory I already found?

Yes. That is often where we start. Send us the factory's name and we will verify it. If it passes, we can help you customize with them.

Q10Do you work on B2B products?

Yes. Most of our work is consumer products, but we also take on B2B devices, kits and components.
